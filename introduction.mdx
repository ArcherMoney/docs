Archer is a rewards platform that lets you automate programmable payouts and physical rewards. Define the payout rules once, and Archer executes them automatically every time.

## What Archer does

Archer brings together a web dashboard and smart contract infrastructure so you can design, control, and run incentive programs at any scale. Here's what you can do:

**Event-driven payouts.** Create payouts that trigger when a condition is met, such as a sales target hit or a milestone reached. Archer monitors conditions and executes payouts automatically within your defined limits.

**On-chain paying limits.** Set maximum amounts per payment, per period, and per number of transactions. These limits aren't just application-level guardrails — they're enforced by the smart contract, so no payout can exceed what you've authorized regardless of how it's triggered.

**Team member management.** Invite recipients by email. Once they complete onboarding and register their wallet, they're ready to receive monetary payouts. You control who is active and can adjust access at any time.

**KYC and KYB compliance.** Before sending payouts, Archer requires identity verification — Know Your Customer (KYC) for individuals and Know Your Business (KYB) for organizations. This keeps your program compliant and your records clean.

<Note>
  Archer is transitioning its login and payout execution layers. Sign-in is moving to a provider-neutral authentication flow, and payout settlement is moving to a managed Modern Treasury backend. References on this site to Privy login, smart-wallet provisioning, and on-chain rule enforcement reflect the previous runtime and will be updated as the new flows ship. See the [changelog](/changelog) for details.
</Note>
