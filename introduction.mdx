Archer is a payments platform that lets you automate programmable payouts to your team members. Instead of manually sending payments each time a goal is hit or a pay period ends, you define the rules once — and Archer executes them automatically, with every transaction enforced by built-in controls for a full audit trail.

## What Archer does

Archer brings together a web dashboard and a secure payments engine so you can design, control, and run incentive programs at any scale. Here's what you can do:

**Event-driven payouts.** Create payouts that trigger when a condition is met — a sales target hit, a milestone reached — Archer monitors conditions and executes payouts automatically within your defined limits.

**Built-in paying limits.** Set maximum amounts per payment, per period, and per number of transactions. These limits aren't just application-level guardrails — they're enforced by Archer's payments engine, so no payout can exceed what you've authorized regardless of how it's triggered.

**Team member management.** Invite recipients by email. Once they complete onboarding and register their payout details, they're ready to receive payouts. You control who is active and can adjust access at any time.

**KYC and KYB compliance.** Before sending payouts, Archer requires identity verification — Know Your Customer (KYC) for individuals and Know Your Business (KYB) for organizations. This keeps your program compliant and your records clean.

<Note>
  All payouts are executed through your Archer payout account. You do not need to manage any keys or credentials — your payout account is provisioned automatically when you sign in.
</Note>
