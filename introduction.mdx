Archer is a rewards platform that helps businesses send gifts, gift cards, and payouts automatically. You set up **campaigns** — natural-language automations that send rewards on your behalf — and Archer fires them whenever a triggering event happens.

Most of Archer lives in the [dashboard](https://archermoney.com). For teams that want to fire campaigns from their own product or internal systems, each campaign comes with a webhook URL you can `POST` events to.

## What you can build with Archer

- **User activation rewards** — gift users the moment they finish onboarding or hit a milestone.
- **Partner and affiliate payouts** — pay partners automatically when a referred deal closes.
- **Employee and contractor incentives** — fire one-off or recurring rewards from internal triggers.
- **Office swag and customer gifts** — send branded goods from Slack or directly from an event.

## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a campaign">
    In the **Campaigns** page, give the campaign a name, a category, and a plain-English description of the reward. Pick a trigger (webhook, Slack, upload, or scraper).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    For webhook campaigns, copy the webhook URL and have your system `POST` events to it. For Slack campaigns, install the Archer Slack app and run the slash command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer sends the reward">
    Every triggering event becomes a reward. Recipients get a branded email with a claim link; approvers can sign off on Office Orders, confirm addresses, and mark shipments sent. Fulfillment shows up in **Activity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
