Archer is a rewards platform for developers. You describe a behavior in plain English ("when a referred user completes onboarding, send a gift card"), point your system at Archer's event endpoint, and Archer runs the reward every time the event fires.

## What you build with Archer

- **User activation rewards** — pay or gift users the moment they finish onboarding, hit an activation milestone, or upgrade.
- **Partner and affiliate payouts** — reward partners automatically when a referred deal closes or a referred user converts.
- **Employee and contractor incentives** — fire one-off or recurring rewards from any internal event (CI pass, ticket closed, NPS submitted).
- **Customer gifts and office orders** — send physical goods, swag, or gift cards from Slack or directly from an event.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define a reward">
    Create a reward rule with a `name`, a plain-English `logic` description of the trigger, a `category` (`customer`, `partner`, or `office`), and a `trigger_kind` (`webhook` or `slack`). See [Rewards](/concepts/rewards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    Archer returns a unique `webhook_url` for each reward. `POST` your events to that URL — Archer ingests them at `/events/{discriminant}` and queues them for processing. See [Events](/concepts/events).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer runs the reward">
    The agent runtime resolves each event to its reward rule and executes idempotently per `(event_id, agent_id)`. Fulfillment shows up on the [Activity](/concepts/activity) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
