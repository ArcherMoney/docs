Archer is a rewards platform that automates sending physical and digital gifts — cake, coffee, swag, donations, and more — to your team, customers, and partners. You define an **agent** with a trigger and category once, and Archer sends the right reward every time the trigger fires.

## What Archer does

Archer pairs a web dashboard with a Slack app so you can design, run, and approve reward programs at any scale.

**Agent-driven rewards.** Create an agent that listens for a webhook, a Slack event, or a schedule. When the trigger fires, the agent sends a reward to the right recipient automatically — no manual work for your team.

**Order approvals.** Configure agents to send automatically or to wait for a human in the loop. Pending orders show up in the Approvals queue, where an owner, admin, or approver can approve or decline before anything ships.

**Activity timeline.** Every reward Archer sends — whether delivered or pending approval — appears in the Activity view, grouped by day and exportable to CSV for reporting.

**Slack integration.** Connect a Slack workspace to let teammates place ad-hoc orders with a slash command. Orders go through the same approval and shipping flow as agent-driven rewards.

**Team and workspace controls.** Invite teammates with role-based access (owner, admin, approver, member), set workspace name and sender details, and store your business address for shipping confirmations.

<Note>
  Archer is designed for sending goods and digital rewards. It does not move funds on a blockchain or hold cryptocurrency on your behalf.
</Note>
