Archer is the **AI bank**. Describe a money movement in plain English — a reward, a payroll run, a savings rule, a spending policy — and Archer wires it up, watches for the triggering event, and moves the money instantly. Anywhere in the world.

Everything starts with an **agent**: a plain-language description of what should happen and when. An Archer AI agent connects to your data, accounts, and logic — no code required — then runs autonomously every time the condition is met.

## What you can automate

- **Rewards** — branded gifts, gift cards, and physical items sent the moment a customer hits a milestone.
- **Payroll** — run scheduled or event-driven payouts to employees and contractors via ACH.
- **Savings rules** — sweep a percentage of every deposit into a savings vault automatically.
- **Affiliate and partner payouts** — pay partners when a referred deal closes or a referred user converts.
- **Performance-based pay** — tie compensation to real outcomes (SDR conversions, UGC purchases, sprint goals shipped) and let Archer execute.
- **Spending policies** — define guardrails in plain English and let Archer enforce them on every transaction.

## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define an automation in plain English">
    Describe any money movement — "Send a branded hat to every user who completes onboarding," "Move 10% of every deposit into a savings vault," "Run payroll every Friday at 5 PM for my team."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer wires it up">
    An Archer AI agent connects to your data, accounts, and logic — no code required. Webhook agents accept events from your product or backend; Slack agents accept slash commands from your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done. Money moves instantly.">
    When the condition is met, money moves — instantly, automatically, every time. Fulfillment shows up in **Activity**; spending and approval guardrails kick in for anything that needs sign-off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Archer fits

Most of Archer lives in the [dashboard](https://archermoney.com), where you create agents, brand recipient experiences, approve internal spend, and watch automations run. For teams that want to fire agents from their own product or backend, every webhook agent gets a unique URL you can `POST` events to.
