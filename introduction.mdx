Archer is a rewards platform that helps businesses send gifts, gift cards, and payouts automatically. You set up **agents** — natural-language automations that send rewards on your behalf — and Archer fires them whenever a triggering event happens.

Most of Archer lives in the [dashboard](https://archermoney.com). For teams that want to fire agents from their own product or internal systems, each agent comes with a webhook URL you can `POST` events to.

## What you can build with Archer

- **User activation rewards** — gift users the moment they finish onboarding or hit a milestone.
- **Partner and affiliate payouts** — pay partners automatically when a referred deal closes.
- **Employee and contractor incentives** — fire one-off or recurring rewards from internal triggers.
- **Office swag and customer gifts** — send branded goods from Slack or directly from an event.

## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    In the **Agents** page, give the agent a name, a category, and a plain-English description of the reward. Pick a trigger (webhook or Slack).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    For webhook agents, copy the agent's webhook URL and have your system `POST` events to it. For Slack agents, install the Archer Slack app and run the slash command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer sends the reward">
    Every triggering event becomes a reward. Recipients get a branded email with a claim link; admins can approve office orders before they ship. Fulfillment shows up in **Activity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
