<Update label="2026-05-21" tags={["New"]} description="Week of May 18">
  ## Subscription billing

  Workspaces can now subscribe to **Basic**, **Pro**, or **Enterprise** from **Settings → Plan**. Checkout and ongoing billing run through Stripe — Archer never handles card data. Owners and admins can upgrade, switch tiers, update payment methods, and cancel from the Stripe customer portal. See [Manage billing and plans](/guides/billing).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-20" tags={["Pivot"]} description="Week of May 18">
  ## Archer is now a rewards platform

  We rebuilt Archer around three things:

  - **Agents** — natural-language automations that send rewards on your behalf.
  - **Webhook and Slack triggers** — fire agents from your product, CRM, or chat.
  - **Approvals** — sign off on Office Orders before they ship.

  The old payouts, smart wallet, and on-/off-ramp surfaces are retired. Start with the new [Introduction](/introduction) and [Quickstart](/quickstart).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-13" tags={["Improvement"]} description="Week of May 11">
  ## Direct agent matching

  Webhook and Slack events now route directly to the agent that owns them — no fuzzy classification across agents in the same workspace. Agents fire faster and predictably, and duplicate events with the same `event_id` are deduped per agent.
</Update>
