<Update label="2026-05-19" tags={["Change"]} description="Week of May 18">
  ## Streamlined rewards product surface

  Archer is now focused on agent-driven rewards: build an agent, point it at a webhook, Slack command, or schedule, and let it ship physical and digital rewards through the Approvals queue and Activity timeline.

  As part of this change, the dashboard and API no longer expose USDC payouts, on-chain spending limits, smart wallet management, fund and off-ramp flows, identity verification (KYC and KYB), CSV imports, or transaction history. Existing customers using those flows should reach out to Archer support before upgrading.

  The current surface area covers:

  - **Agents** with `webhook`, `slack`, and `schedule` triggers, plus `office`, `customer`, and `partner` categories.
  - **Activity** with `delivered` and `pending` statuses, grouped by day, with CSV export.
  - **Approvals** with role-based access (`owner`, `admin`, `approver`).
  - **Team** invites and role management (`owner`, `admin`, `approver`, `member`).
  - **Slack** install, slash-command ordering, and disconnect.
  - **Notifications** for approval emails, weekly summaries, and daily digests.
  - **Workspace settings** for sender name, reply-to email, and structured business address.

  [Read the introduction](/introduction) for the new tour, or jump to the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-14" tags={["Feature"]} description="Week of May 11">
  ## Integrations page with Slack

  The Slack settings page is now **Integrations**, a single place to manage every workspace-level connection for your Archer account. Slack is the first integration available — install it from **Settings → Integrations** to use the `/order` slash command from any channel. If you run a slash command before connecting, Slack now replies with a clear prompt that points you to the Integrations page instead of a generic error. [Learn more](/guides/connect-slack)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-14" tags={["Feature"]} description="Week of May 11">
  ## Choose how to deliver a Slack order

  When you place an order from Slack, Archer now asks how you want it delivered. Pick **Send Order Form** to email an order form to a recipient, or **Send to Address** to ship the item directly to a street address. Archer collects only the fields that match your choice — an email for order forms, or a full shipping address for direct delivery — and confirms the submission in Slack. [Learn more](/guides/place-slack-order)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-15" tags={["Improvement"]} description="Week of May 11">
  ## Slack source label on agent cards

  Agent cards on the Agents page now show **Slack** with a Slack icon for Slack-backed agents, and **Webhook** with the bolt icon for webhook-triggered agents. Use this at-a-glance label to see which integration feeds each agent without opening the agent details.
</Update>
