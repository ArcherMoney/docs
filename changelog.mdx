<Update label="2026-07-30" tags={["New"]} description="Week of July 27">
  ## New: embed the worker view with `@archermoney/sdk`

  Partners can now drop the Archer worker balance, activity, and cash-out surface directly into their own app with the new [`@archermoney/sdk`](/developers/embed-workers-sdk) package. Your app hands the SDK a worker JWT; Archer exchanges it for a scoped worker session and renders the recipient view against the worker's own wallet.

  - Headless `ArcherWorkerClient` for token exchange, account and transaction reads, Bridge KYC, and cash-out prepare/submit.
  - `ArcherWorkerView` React component (plus `ArcherWorkerProvider` and `useArcherWorker` for custom layouts).
  - `mountArcherWorkerWidget` for non-React pages and script-tag embeds.

  Embed sessions are recipient-only — they cannot call sender or admin routes. Ask Archer to add your partner origins to the CORS allowlist before you go live.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-27" tags={["New", "Improvement"]} description="Week of July 20">
  ## New: fund and cash out in more currencies

  Workspaces can now fund their Archer wallet from EUR, GBP, MXN, BRL, and COP bank accounts in addition to USD. Deposits convert to USDC on Base automatically, and the **Deposit → Bank transfer** panel provisions a virtual account per currency you enable.

  Cash-out grew the same way. Workspace admins and claimed recipients can pay out to local bank accounts in:

  - **EUR** over SEPA
  - **GBP** over Faster Payments
  - **MXN** over SPEI
  - **BRL** over Pix
  - **COP** over Bre-B

  Pick a payout currency in the **Cash out** panel and Archer routes the transfer over the matching local rail.

  ## New: invite workers to claim their own account

  A new **Invite** action on the Workers page sends a one-time claim link. Once the worker signs in, Archer provisions a self-custodied wallet bound to their login and drops them into a recipient dashboard with balance, activity, and cash-out. Invite status (**Pending**, **Claimed**) shows on the roster, and you can regenerate or revoke pending links.

  ## Improved: API signer replaces JWT token exchange

  Programmatic access to the People API now uses an **API signer** — a scoped EVM key pair you generate on the **Developers** page and use to sign each request with EIP-191. No more JWKS setup, token-exchange round trips, or 5-minute bearer tokens.

  - Give the signer a **name** so you can tell rotations apart.
  - Keys are generated **in your browser** — Archer stores only the public address and scopes.
  - Scopes (`person:read`, `person:write`) still gate what a signer can do.

  Existing JWT/OAuth token-exchange integrations should migrate to signers.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-20" tags={["Pivot"]} description="Week of May 18">
  ## Archer is now a rewards platform

  We rebuilt Archer around three things:

  - **Agents** — natural-language automations that send rewards on your behalf.
  - **Webhook and Slack triggers** — fire agents from your product, CRM, or chat.
  - **Approvals** — sign off on Office Orders before they ship.

  The old payouts, smart wallet, and on-/off-ramp surfaces are retired. Start with the new [Introduction](/introduction) and [Quickstart](/quickstart).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-13" tags={["Improvement"]} description="Week of May 11">
  ## Direct agent matching

  Webhook and Slack events now route directly to the agent that owns them — no fuzzy classification across agents in the same workspace. Agents fire faster and predictably, and duplicate events with the same `event_id` are deduped per agent.
</Update>
