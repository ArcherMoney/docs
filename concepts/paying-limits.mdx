Paying Limits let you define a budget envelope that Archer enforces every time a payout is attempted. These limits are not soft application-layer checks — they are configured in Archer's payments engine and evaluated before each transfer executes. A transaction that exceeds any limit is rejected; no funds move.

## The three limit dimensions

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Max per payment" icon="circle-dollar-sign">
    The highest USD amount that can be sent in a single payout transaction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Max per period" icon="chart-bar">
    The total USD that can be paid out across all transactions within the configured period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Max transactions per period" icon="arrows-left-right">
    The number of payout calls allowed within the configured period.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Configuring a period

All three limits share a single time window. You define this window by entering a number and selecting a unit:

| Unit | Duration |
|---|---|
| Hours | 3,600 seconds per unit |
| Days | 86,400 seconds per unit |
| Weeks | 604,800 seconds per unit |
| Months | 2,592,000 seconds per unit |

For example, setting the period to `2 Weeks` means the per-period limits reset every two weeks, starting from the moment the first payout in that window executes.

## How enforcement works

When a payout is triggered, Archer's payments engine checks all three limits before transferring any funds:

1. Is the payout amount within your max per payment?
2. Would this payout push the period total above your max per period?
3. Would this payout exceed your max transactions per period for the current window?

If any check fails, the entire transaction is rejected with a specific error. Nothing is deducted and the payout is not retried automatically.

<Warning>
Saving your Paying Limits applies the new rules to your account. Allow a few seconds for the changes to take effect. If you close the page before confirmation, your changes may not be saved.
</Warning>
