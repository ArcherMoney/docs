Archer sends notifications when something on your account needs your attention or crosses a threshold you care about. Notifications are grouped into two categories — **Approvals** and **Spending** — and each category can be delivered over email or mobile push. This page explains what each category covers and how delivery is decided.

## Categories

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Approvals | Actions on your account that need a confirmation from you, such as confirming a recipient restore. |
| Spending | Activity tied to spending thresholds and card transactions on your account. |

Archer always shows in-app notifications regardless of your settings. Email and push are opt-in per category.

## The in-app notification feed

Every page in the Archer web app has a bell icon in the top bar. The bell is your in-app feed for both approval and spending notifications.

- **Unread badge** — when you have unread notifications, the bell shows a count next to it. Counts greater than nine are displayed as `9+`.
- **Recent notifications** — selecting the bell opens a dropdown with your most recent notifications. Each row shows a title, an optional body, and a dot indicating whether it has been read.
- **Mark all read** — the dropdown header has a **Mark all read** button that clears every unread notification on your account in one action. The button is disabled when nothing is unread.

The feed shows up to the 20 most recent notifications by default and refreshes every time you open the bell. In-app notifications stay visible after they are read so you can refer back to them — marking a notification read only clears the unread badge and the dot.

## Channels

Each category supports two configurable delivery channels:

- **Email** — sent to the email address on your Archer account.
- **Push** — sent to the Archer mobile app on devices signed in to your account.

<Note>
  Mobile push delivery is not yet live. You can toggle push preferences today and Archer will store them, but push messages will only start arriving once mobile delivery ships in a later release. Email is delivered now.
</Note>

## Default preferences

The first time Archer needs to notify you, it creates a notification settings record on your account with these defaults:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Approvals — email | On |
| Approvals — push | Off |
| Spending — email | On |
| Spending — push | Off |

You can change any of these at any time from **Settings → Notifications**. See [Manage notification preferences](/guides/manage-notifications) for the step-by-step flow.

## How Archer decides delivery

When Archer enqueues a notification, it checks your settings for the matching category and only sends over channels you have enabled. For example, if you turn **Approvals — email** off, an approval notification will still appear in-app but no email will go out. Turning everything off for a category silences email and push for that category but leaves in-app notifications intact.

<Tip>
  Keep **Approvals — email** on if you want a written record of every confirmation step. Approval notifications include a description of the pending action and, where applicable, a link back to Archer to resolve it.
</Tip>
