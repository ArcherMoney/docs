Archer sends email notifications when something on your account needs your attention or summarizes recent activity. You control delivery per account from **Settings → Notifications**.

## Categories

Each notification belongs to one of three categories. You can turn email delivery on or off per category — there is no separate push channel today.

| Category | When it sends |
|---|---|
| **Approval needed** | A new order lands in [Approvals](/concepts/approvals) and is waiting on your sign-off. Best left on for anyone with the `owner`, `admin`, or `approver` role. |
| **Weekly summary** | A Monday digest of every reward your agents sent in the previous seven days, grouped by agent and category. |
| **Daily digest** | One quiet daily summary at end-of-day, instead of a notification per event. Useful for owners who want a single pulse-check email. |

## Defaults

New accounts start with:

- **Approval needed** — on
- **Weekly summary** — off
- **Daily digest** — off

The defaults are designed so a brand-new workspace surfaces the email you actually need to act on (approvals) without dropping a stream of summary emails into your inbox.

## Per-member preferences

Notification settings are per account member. Every signed-in user can toggle their own preferences and only sees their own settings — there is no global override. Owners and admins can view their own preferences but cannot change another member's settings on their behalf.
