Team members in Archer are the people who can receive payouts. The Recipients page lists every payee you've added, including both **Archer** recipients and **External** recipients. Archer recipients are smart wallet holders you've invited and activated on-chain. External recipients are traditional US bank accounts you've added for ACH or wire payouts. This page explains each lifecycle stage and how to manage both recipient types.

## Recipient sources

Each row on the Recipients page shows a source pill that tells you where the recipient receives funds:

| Source | Description |
|---|---|
| **Archer** | A smart wallet recipient who completed Archer onboarding and was activated on-chain. Receives USDC. |
| **External** | A US bank account you added directly. Receives ACH or wire payouts. Skips the invite, onboarding, and on-chain activation steps. |

External recipients display masked bank details — only the last four digits of the account and routing numbers are ever returned by the API or rendered in the dashboard.

## Archer member lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite">
    Enter the recipient's email address in the Team Members page and click **Send Invite**. Archer sends them an invitation email and creates a record with status `invited`. You can also import multiple recipients at once using a CSV file — see the **Import CSV** button in the top toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarded">
    The recipient clicks the invite link, signs up, and completes their profile. Once their account is created and their smart wallet address is registered, their status automatically updates to `onboarded`. No action is required from you at this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    When a member shows the **Activate** button in the Actions column, their profile is ready. Click **Activate** to register their wallet address on-chain as an approved payout recipient. This calls the `setRecipient` function on the ArcherAgentEndpoint contract, adding the wallet to your allowlist. The member's status updates to `active` once the transaction confirms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive">
    To remove someone from your active recipient set, click the archive icon next to their name. If the member is currently `active`, Archer removes their wallet address from the on-chain allowlist before archiving the record. Archived members are not deleted — you can view and restore them via **Archived Members**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Status badges

Each team member displays a status badge that reflects where they are in the lifecycle:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `invited` | Invite has been sent; recipient has not yet signed up |
| `onboarded` | Recipient has completed signup and their wallet is registered |
| `active` | Wallet is registered on-chain; member can receive payouts |
| `archived` | Member has been removed from the active recipient set |

## External bank account recipients

To send payouts to a US bank account instead of an on-chain wallet, add the payee as an **External** recipient. From the Recipients page, click **Add by Bank Account** and fill in the recipient's name, ACH account and routing numbers, and US billing address. The routing number must be exactly nine digits.

External recipients are created instantly — there is no invite email, no onboarding flow, and no on-chain activation. They appear in the Recipients table immediately with an **External** source pill and are ready to receive ACH or wire payouts.

<Tip>
Archer never returns full bank account or routing numbers after they are saved. The Recipients page and the `/fiat_recipient` API only expose the last four digits of each value. Store the originals in a secure system of record if you need to retrieve them later.
</Tip>

## Bulk import via CSV

If you need to add many Archer wallet recipients at once, use the **Import CSV** button in the Recipients header. Your CSV must include a `name` column and a `wallet_address` column for each row. An `email` column is optional but recommended. Recipients imported this way are created with `active` status immediately — no separate activation step is required. CSV import is for on-chain recipients only; add bank accounts one at a time using **Add by Bank Account**.

<Tip>
Only Archer team members with `active` status can receive on-chain payouts. Their wallet address must be registered on the ArcherAgentEndpoint contract before any USDC transfer will succeed. External recipients bypass this check because they are paid through ACH or wire rails instead of the smart contract.
</Tip>
