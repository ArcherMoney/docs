Team members are the people who can sign in to your Archer workspace. Each member has a role that controls what they can see and do. You manage members and pending invites from **Settings → Team**.

## Roles

Archer uses four role names. The role you assign at invite time determines what the member can do on day one — you can change it later.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Full control. Workspace settings, Slack install/update/delete, agent create/update/delete, team member edits, invites, and approvals. |
| `admin` | Same as owner for day-to-day operations: workspace settings, Slack, agents, team management, notification settings, and approvals. |
| `approver` | Read access plus the ability to approve or decline pending orders in [Approvals](/concepts/approvals). Cannot edit agents or invite people. |
| `member` | Read-only access to account, agents, activity, team list, Slack connection, and their own notification preferences. |

The dashboard hides controls that the signed-in member does not have permission to use, so a `member` simply will not see destructive buttons or the Approvals tab.

## Invites

Inviting a teammate creates a pending user record with the requested email and role. The invitee receives a sign-in link; once they accept it, the pending record is promoted to an active member with the role you chose.

You can:

- **Resend** an invite if the recipient lost the email.
- **Cancel** a pending invite before it is accepted.
- **Change** a member's role at any time from the Team tab.

## Member lifecycle

Every member is either pending (invited but not yet signed in) or active. Removing an active member revokes their access immediately — they will be signed out on their next request and will need a fresh invite to come back.
