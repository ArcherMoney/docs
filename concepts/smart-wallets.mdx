When you sign up for Archer, a payout account is created for you automatically. Understanding how it works helps you know where your funds live, who authorizes transactions, and how Archer's payments engine fits into each payout.

## Your payout account

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sign-in identity" icon="key">
    Your account is tied to your sign-in identity, established the first time you log in. Archer uses this identity to authorize sensitive actions — including activating team members and updating paying limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payout account" icon="wallet">
    A dedicated account provisioned on your behalf. This is the account that holds the funds used for payouts. Its details appear in **Settings** as **Payout Account**. When a payout executes, funds are transferred out of this account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How payouts flow through Archer

Archer's payments engine sits between your payout account and your recipients. Every time a payout fires, the engine:

1. Verifies the recipient is on your approved allowlist
2. Checks that the transfer amount is within your configured paying limits (max per payment, max per period, max transactions per period)
3. Executes the transfer from your payout account to the recipient

If any of those checks fail, the transaction is rejected and no funds are moved. This means your spending rules and recipient allowlist are enforced at the payments-engine level, not just by the Archer application.

## Viewing your account

Your payout account details are visible in **Settings** under **Payout Account**.

You do not need to do anything to set this up. Your payout account is provisioned automatically the first time you sign in to Archer.

<Note>
All payouts — whether in production or a test environment — are real transactions executed by Archer's payments engine.
</Note>
