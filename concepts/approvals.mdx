Approvals is the queue of rewards waiting on a human review. When an agent or Slack order requires sign-off, the order lands here in `pending` state. An approver can approve it (the order ships) or decline it (the order is dropped and hidden from [Activity](/concepts/activity)).

## When orders need approval

An order ends up in Approvals when the agent that produced it — or the Slack flow that placed it — is configured to require a human in the loop. Automatic agents bypass Approvals and go straight to fulfillment.

## Who can act on the queue

Approval actions are governed by role-based access:

| Role | Can view queue | Can approve or decline |
|---|---|---|
| `owner` | ✓ | ✓ |
| `admin` | ✓ | ✓ |
| `approver` | ✓ | ✓ |
| `member` | ✗ | ✗ |

If you do not have a qualifying role, the **Approvals** entry in the sidebar is hidden. See [Invite team members](/guides/invite-team-members) for how to assign roles.

## What you see in the queue

Each Approvals row shows:

- The reward and any variant the order picked (for example, `cake · vanilla · 8"`).
- The recipient and where it will ship.
- The agent or Slack user that placed the order.
- The time the order was created.

Approving moves the order to `fulfilled`, where it appears in Activity as `delivered`. Declining drops the order entirely — declined orders are not returned by the queue and are not surfaced in Activity.

## Notifications

Account members with the **Approval needed** preference enabled receive an email whenever a new order lands in Approvals. See [Manage notifications](/guides/manage-notifications) to toggle this.
