When you place a goods order through Slack or an agent, Archer does not buy the item immediately. The order moves through a short workflow that ends with you confirming the vendor, product link, and price before any funds leave your account. This page explains each status and what triggers the next one.

## The approval workflow

A goods order passes through five statuses from creation to fulfillment:

| Status | What it means | Who moves it forward |
|---|---|---|
| `pending` | The order exists but is waiting on shipping details from the recipient. | Recipient (via the emailed order form) or the requester (if a shipping address was provided upfront). |
| `submitted` | Shipping details are complete and the order is ready to be priced. | Archer purchasing team |
| `pending_approval` | The purchasing team has attached a vendor, product URL, and price. The order is waiting on your approval. | You, the account owner |
| `approved` | You approved the vendor, link, and price. The order is cleared for purchase. | Archer purchasing team |
| `fulfilled` | The purchasing team completed the buy and the matching transaction was posted to your account. | — |

<Note>
  Approval always happens in the Archer product frontend at **Approvals**, not in Slack or email. Archer routes every order through this gate so a person on your account confirms the final amount before it is charged.
</Note>

## What you see at approval time

Each pending approval shows three pieces of information drawn from the quoted order:

- **Item** — the original request from Slack or your agent.
- **Vendor** — the seller the purchasing team plans to buy from.
- **Price** — the exact USD amount that will be charged when the order is fulfilled.

Approving the order locks in that price. If the purchasing team needs to change the vendor, URL, or price after you have approved, the order is moved back to `pending_approval` and you are asked to approve again.

## Why orders may be re-quoted

The purchasing team can edit a quote while the order is in `pending_approval` or `approved`. If any of `price`, `vendor`, or `url` change on an already-approved order, Archer automatically reverts the order to `pending_approval` so the new quote goes through the same approval gate. Fulfillment is blocked until you re-approve.

## How fulfillment uses the approved price

Fulfillment uses the price stored on the order at the moment of approval — Archer does not accept a separate amount at fulfillment time. This means:

- An order with no price set cannot be fulfilled.
- Editing the price after approval forces another approval round before the order can be fulfilled.
- The amount that appears in your transaction history matches the approved price exactly.
