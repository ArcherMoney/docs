Three pages in the dashboard tell you what's happening with your rewards: **Activity**, **Approvals**, and **Orders**.

## Order lifecycle

Goods orders move through a fixed set of statuses. Each one corresponds to a step in the dashboard:

| Status | Where it appears | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `PENDING_APPROVAL` | Approvals | A campaign created the order; an approver needs to sign off. |
| `PENDING_CLAIM` | (recipient's inbox) | Approved. Archer emailed the recipient a form to enter their shipping address. |
| `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` | Approvals | The recipient submitted (or the campaign provided) a shipping address; an approver needs to confirm it. |
| `PENDING_FULFILLMENT` | Orders | Address confirmed. Ready for your team to ship. |
| `SENT` | Activity | Your team marked the order shipped. |
| `CANCELLED` | (hidden) | An approver cancelled the order before fulfillment. Not shown in Activity. |

Customer Gifts and Partner Gifts that don't involve a physical good skip the Approvals and Orders steps and go straight to `SENT` when the reward settles.

## Activity

The **Activity** page shows every reward that's gone out, grouped by day. Each row links back to the campaign that fired it and the recipient. There's a single status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sent` | The reward shipped or settled to the recipient. |

In-progress and cancelled orders are not shown here — see Approvals or Orders for those.

### Filters and export

- **Date range** — narrow Activity to a specific period.
- **Export** — download the filtered view as CSV. Useful for accounting, finance, and end-of-quarter reporting.

## Approvals

The **Approvals** page lists goods orders waiting on a decision. Each row shows the campaign that fired it, the requested item, the recipient, and the quoted price.

Anyone with the **owner**, **admin**, or **approver** role can act:

- **Approve** an order in `PENDING_APPROVAL` — the order moves to `PENDING_CLAIM` and Archer emails the recipient the address form. (For direct-address campaigns the order skips this step.)
- **Confirm address** on an order in `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` — the order moves to `PENDING_FULFILLMENT` and shows up on the Orders page.
- **Cancel** any pending order — the order moves to `CANCELLED` and is removed from the queue.

Customer Gifts and Partner Gifts that don't ship a physical good don't appear here.

## Orders

The **Orders** page is the fulfillment queue. It lists every order in `PENDING_FULFILLMENT` — addresses confirmed, ready to ship.

Each row shows the item, recipient, vendor, quoted price, and confirmed shipping address. When your team has shipped the order, click **Mark sent**. The order moves to `SENT` and shows up on Activity.

<Tip>
  Use Orders as your daily shipping worklist. Filter Approvals when you need to sign off; come to Orders when you have a stack of packages to send out.
</Tip>

## Who sees what

| Role | Activity | Approvals | Orders |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Read + export | Approve, confirm, cancel | Mark sent |
| Admin | Read + export | Approve, confirm, cancel | Mark sent |
| Approver | Read + export | Approve, confirm, cancel | Mark sent |
| Member | Read + export | Read only | Read only |

## Recent activity on each campaign

The campaign detail page also shows the last few rewards that campaign fired. It's the fastest way to confirm a new campaign is wired up correctly — fire a test gift from the simulator and watch it appear.
