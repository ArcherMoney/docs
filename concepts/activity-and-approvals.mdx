title: "Activity & approvals"
description: "How Archer surfaces every reward — the Activity feed, the Approvals queue for Office Orders, and the statuses you'll see."
---

Two pages in the dashboard tell you what's happening with your rewards: **Activity** and **Approvals**.

## Activity

The **Activity** page shows every reward Archer has produced, grouped by day. Each row links back to the agent that fired it and the recipient. You'll see two statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | The reward is in the Approvals queue waiting on a decision. Only Office Orders land here. |
| `delivered` | The reward has been shipped or settled to the recipient. |

Declined Office Orders are hidden from Activity (they're visible in the Approvals history if you need to audit them).

### Filters and export

- **Date range** — narrow Activity to a specific period.
- **Status** — show only `pending` or only `delivered`.
- **Export** — download the filtered view as CSV. Useful for accounting, finance, and end-of-quarter reporting.

## Approvals

The **Approvals** page lists Office Orders waiting on someone to act. Each row shows the agent that fired it, the requested item, the recipient, and the quoted price.

Anyone with the **owner**, **admin**, or **approver** role can:

- **Approve** — the order moves to fulfillment and eventually shows up as `delivered` in Activity.
- **Decline** — the order is removed from the queue and hidden from Activity.
- **Approve all** — bulk-approve the entire queue.

Customer Gifts and Partner Gifts don't appear here — they send immediately when their agent fires.

## Who sees what

| Role | Activity | Approvals |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Read + export | Approve, decline, approve all |
| Admin | Read + export | Approve, decline, approve all |
| Approver | Read + export | Approve, decline, approve all |
| Member | Read + export | Read only |

## Recent activity on each agent

The agent detail page also shows the last few rewards that agent fired. It's the fastest way to confirm a new agent is wired up correctly — fire a test gift from the simulator and watch it appear.
