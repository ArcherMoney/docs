Every campaign has a **category**. The category controls how the reward is delivered and whether anyone on your team has to approve it before it goes out.

## The three categories

### Customer Gift

A reward sent to an end user — typically a gift card or branded item. Archer emails the recipient a claim link with your campaign's branding (subject, body, color, logo). The recipient picks their gift or fills in a shipping address, and Archer ships or settles directly.

**Use it for**: onboarding gifts, milestone rewards, NPS thank-yous, churn save offers.

### Partner Gift

A payout to a partner, affiliate, or contractor — usually triggered when a referred outcome happens (a deal closes, a referred user converts). Archer routes to the partner's saved payout method.

**Use it for**: affiliate commissions, referral payouts, contractor bonuses.

### Office Order

An internal reward — swag, supplies, or gifts for employees. Office Orders run through the full goods-order lifecycle: **Approvals** (approve, confirm address) then **Orders** (mark sent). Owners, admins, and approvers can act at each step.

**Use it for**: new-hire kits, employee gifts, office supplies fired from Slack.

## Comparison

| Category | Needs approval | Recipient sees | Common trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Gift | No | Branded claim email | Webhook from your product |
| Partner Gift | No | Branded claim email or direct payout | Webhook from your CRM |
| Office Order | Yes (admin / approver) | Branded claim email + shipping form | Slack slash command |

## Switching categories

You can change a campaign's category from the Campaigns page at any time. The change takes effect for new events only — orders already in the Approvals or Orders queue keep their original routing.
