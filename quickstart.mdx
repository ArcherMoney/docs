title: "Quickstart: send your first reward with Archer"
description: "Create your workspace, connect Slack or a webhook, build your first agent, and ship your first reward — all in about ten minutes."
---

This guide walks you from a blank account to your first reward going out the door. The whole flow takes about ten minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and create your workspace">
    Go to [app.archermoney.com](https://app.archermoney.com) and create an account. Set your **workspace name**, **sender display name**, **reply-to email**, and **business address** under **Settings → General**. Recipients see the sender name on every reward email, and the business address is used on shipping confirmations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Settings → Team** and invite teammates by email. Choose a role for each invite:

    - **Owner** or **Admin** — full workspace control.
    - **Approver** — can approve or decline pending orders.
    - **Member** — read-only access to activity and agents.

    See [Invite team members](/guides/invite-team-members) for the full role matrix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a trigger source">
    Decide how your first agent will fire:

    - **Webhook** — paste the agent's webhook URL into any tool that sends HTTP POSTs (Zapier, Segment, your CRM, your own backend).
    - **Slack** — install the Archer Slack app from **Settings → Integrations**. See [Connect Slack](/guides/connect-slack).
    - **Schedule** — pick a daily, weekly, quarterly, or annual cadence at agent-creation time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first agent">
    Open **Agents** and click **New agent**. Give it a name, write a plain-language **logic** statement (the condition that should trigger it), pick a **category** — `office`, `customer`, or `partner` — and choose a reward. Save the agent.

    See [Create an agent](/guides/create-agent) for a walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and approve">
    Fire the trigger once (send a test webhook, run the Slack command, or wait for the scheduled run). The order appears in **Activity** as `pending` if it needs review, or `delivered` once it ships. Approve pending orders from **Approvals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
