This guide gets a working agent live in your account. You'll do everything from the dashboard — no code required.

## Prerequisites

- An Archer account at [archermoney.com](https://archermoney.com). New accounts are provisioned as Business workspaces on first sign-in via Auth0 (email, Google, or Microsoft).
- About five minutes.

## 1. Sign in and finish workspace setup

Open the dashboard and complete the first-time setup prompts. At minimum, set:

- **Workspace name** — shown internally.
- **Sender display name** — what recipients see as the sender on reward emails.
- **Reply-to email** — where recipient replies go.
- **Business address** — used for fulfillment.

You can change all of these later under **Settings**.

## 2. Create your first agent

Open the **Agents** page and click **New agent**. Fill out:

- **Name** — a short label (e.g. "Onboarding gift").
- **Category** — `Office Order`, `Customer Gift`, or `Partner Gift`. This decides how the reward is fulfilled and whether it needs approval.
- **Logic** — a one- or two-sentence description of when the reward fires and what to send. Archer matches incoming events against this description.
- **Trigger** — every agent uses a **Webhook** trigger. Archer generates a unique URL when you save.

Save. The agent appears in the list with **Active** status.

## 3. Send a test gift

On the agent's detail page, scroll to **Simulate agent**. Enter your own email under **Recipient email** and click **Send test →**.

Archer sends a real reward email to that address as if the agent had fired. You'll see the agent's branding (subject line, body, color, optional "Powered by Archer" toggle) and the claim flow your recipients will go through.

<Tip>
  Use the simulator any time you change an agent's branding or category. It's the fastest way to sanity-check what recipients will actually receive.
</Tip>

## 4. Wire up the real trigger (optional)

If you want your product or backend to fire this agent automatically, copy the agent's **Webhook URL** from the detail page and have your system `POST` events to it. See [Sending events](/developers/sending-events) for the request shape and best practices.
