## How Archer works

Archer combines a web dashboard with a Slack app to automate sending rewards — without the manual coordination of picking, ordering, and shipping.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your workspace">
    Sign up and fill in your workspace name, sender display name, reply-to email, and business address. Recipients see the sender on every reward and the business address on shipping confirmations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add teammates with role-based access — `owner`, `admin`, `approver`, or `member`. Roles control who can edit agents, approve orders, or just read activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build agents">
    Create agents that listen for webhooks, Slack commands, or schedules. Each agent has a plain-language logic statement, a category (`office`, `customer`, or `partner`), and a reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and track">
    Orders that need a human in the loop land in **Approvals**. Approved orders ship, and every reward appears in **Activity** for reporting and CSV export.
  </Step>
</Steps>
