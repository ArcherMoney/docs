Archer helps businesses send gifts, gift cards, and payouts automatically. Create a campaign in the dashboard, describe in plain English what should fire it, and Archer takes care of the rest — branded emails, recipient claim flow, approvals, and fulfillment.

## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a campaign in the dashboard">
    Name it, pick a category, write the logic in plain English, and choose a webhook, Slack, upload, or scraper trigger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    Copy the campaign's webhook URL into your product, or install Slack and run `/archer` in any channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer sends the reward">
    Recipients get a branded claim email. Office Orders flow through Approvals and Orders before shipping.
  </Step>
</Steps>
