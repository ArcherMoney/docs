Archer helps businesses send gifts, gift cards, and payouts automatically. Create an agent in the dashboard, describe in plain English what should fire it, and Archer takes care of the rest — branded emails, recipient claim flow, approvals, and fulfillment.

## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an agent in the dashboard">
    Name it, pick a category, write the logic in plain English, and choose a webhook or Slack trigger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    Copy the agent's webhook URL into your product, or install Slack and run `/archer` in any channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer sends the reward">
    Recipients get a branded claim email or, for Office Orders, the request lands in your Approvals queue first.
  </Step>
</Steps>
