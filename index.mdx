## How Archer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define a reward">
    Describe the trigger and reward in plain English. Pick a category (`office`, `customer`, `partner`) and a trigger kind (webhook or Slack).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up the trigger">
    Archer gives each reward a unique webhook URL. `POST` your events to it from any system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archer runs the reward">
    The agent runtime resolves each event to its reward and executes idempotently. Fulfillment shows up on the Activity page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
