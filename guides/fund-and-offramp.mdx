Archer holds your payout funds in a smart wallet — a blockchain-based account that the Archer agent draws from when executing payouts. The Dashboard gives you a real-time view of that balance and provides controls to add funds or move money back to a traditional bank account.

## View your balance

The Dashboard displays your current **USDC balance** in your payout smart wallet as the first item on the page. The balance is read directly from the blockchain and reflects funds that are available for payouts right now.

## Deposit funds

Click **Deposit** on the Dashboard to open the Deposit modal. The modal offers three ways to move funds into your Archer balance.

### Linked accounts

Connect a US checking account once and reuse it for instant deposits.

### Deposit cash (manual bank transfer)

Use the manual rails when you want to push funds from any external bank — for example, a corporate treasury account that you do not want to link via Plaid.

1. Expand **Deposit Cash** in the Deposit modal.
2. Click the eye icon to reveal your dedicated **Routing number** and **Account number**.
3. Copy the values into your bank's ACH or wire transfer form and initiate the transfer.

These bank details are unique to your Archer account and are generated by Archer's banking partner. Funds sent to these details are credited as USDC to your smart wallet after settlement.

<Note>
  Your bank details are provisioned automatically after onboarding. If you see a "still being provisioned" message, wait a minute and reopen the modal.
</Note>

### Crypto account

Send USDC directly from any wallet on Base to your Archer smart wallet.

1. Expand **Crypto Account** in the Deposit modal.
2. Click the eye icon to reveal your **Wallet ID** (smart wallet address on Base).
3. Copy the address and send USDC on Base from any external wallet. Funds land in your Archer balance once the transaction confirms on-chain.

<Warning>
  Only send USDC on the Base network to this address. Other assets or networks may be unrecoverable.
</Warning>

## Send funds

Click **Send** on the Dashboard to open the Send modal. The modal has two tabs.

### Send to a recipient

Pay any team member that you have already added in **Team members**.

1. Use the search field to filter recipients by name or email.
2. Select a recipient. Archer loads their default payout bank automatically.
3. Enter the amount in USD.
4. Click **Send** to confirm. Archer transfers USDC from your smart wallet, then issues an ACH off-ramp to the recipient's bank.

If a recipient has no linked payout bank, the Send button is disabled until they connect one.

### Send to my account

Withdraw funds from your Archer balance back to one of your own linked bank accounts.

1. Switch to the **Send to my account** tab.
2. Pick a linked account from the list. Add a new one with **+ Link new account** if needed.
3. Enter the amount and click **Send**.

Archer sends USDC from your smart wallet and initiates an ACH transfer to the selected bank account.

<Note>
  Off-ramp transactions — both recipient and personal — may take 1–3 business days to settle depending on your bank and the ACH network. The status shown in Archer reflects when Archer submitted the payment order, not when your bank posts the funds.
</Note>

## Track pending deposits

Once you initiate a deposit, it appears in the **Pending deposits** section at the top of the Activity page. Each row shows the payment type (ACH or Wire), the amount, and the destination account. An amber status indicator describes where the deposit is in the settlement flow:

- **Sent** — the payment order has been submitted to the bank network
- **Settling** — the funds are clearing and will arrive shortly
- **Processing** — the deposit is in an intermediate state

The section disappears automatically once a deposit completes. If a deposit fails — for example, because the payment order could not be created — its status updates to **Failed** and the entry leaves **Pending deposits**. You can review failed deposits in your transaction history.
