---
title: "Fund your Archer wallet with a bank transfer"
description: "Verify your identity, set up a US virtual account, and deposit USD by ACH or wire. Archer converts your deposit to USDC on Base automatically."
---

Fund your Archer wallet in US dollars from any US bank account. Archer provisions a dedicated virtual account for your workspace, and every deposit is converted to USDC on Base and credited to your smart wallet.

Use bank transfers when you want to top up your workspace balance to pay for rewards, payroll, or partner payouts without holding crypto yourself.

## Prerequisites

- You're signed in to the [dashboard](https://archermoney.com) as an **Owner** or **Admin**.
- Your smart wallet is provisioned (this happens automatically on first sign-in).
- A US bank account you can send ACH or wire from.

## 1. Verify your identity

Bank transfers require a completed identity check the first time you fund your workspace. This is a one-time step per workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deposit">
    Click **Deposit** in the top bar, then choose **Bank transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start verification">
    Click **Verify to add funds**. A verification flow opens with our banking partner, Bridge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your details">
    Provide the requested personal or business information and any supporting documents. Most individual checks clear in a few minutes; business verification can take longer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You only need to verify once. When your status flips to **Approved**, the bank transfer panel unlocks.

## 2. Set up your virtual account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deposit → Bank transfer">
    Click **Deposit** and switch to **Bank transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Set up bank transfer">
    Archer provisions a US virtual account in your workspace's name. This usually takes a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your deposit details">
    Copy or download the bank name, beneficiary name, account number, and routing number. These stay the same for every future deposit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Send a deposit

From your bank's app or portal, initiate an ACH or wire transfer to the virtual account.

- **Amount** — minimum **$1**. Deposits settle to the whole cent.
- **Reference / memo** — paste the **Reference** shown in the Bank transfer panel exactly as displayed. Archer uses it to match the incoming transfer to your workspace.

<Warning>
  If the reference is missing or wrong, your deposit will be held for manual review, which can delay funds by several days.
</Warning>

## 4. Track your deposit

Once your bank sends the funds, the **Latest deposit** row in the Bank transfer panel updates in real time. It also shows up in the **Activity** feed once completed.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Your bank has initiated the transfer. It can take a few minutes to reach us. |
| **Received** | We received the funds and are converting them to USDC. |
| **In review** | The deposit is under compliance review. We'll email you when it clears. |
| **Sending** | USDC is being sent to your smart wallet on Base. |
| **Completed** | USDC has landed in your wallet. The deposit also appears in Activity. |
| **Refunding** | We're returning this deposit to your originating bank. |
| **Refunded** | The deposit was returned to your bank. |
| **Refund failed** | We couldn't return this deposit. [Contact support](mailto:support@archermoney.com). |

## Tips

- Reuse the same virtual account for every deposit — you don't need to set it up again.
- Deposits are one-way. To move funds back out, use payroll or payout agents rather than reversing a deposit.
- Only send USD from a bank account in the workspace owner's or business's name. Third-party transfers may be held or returned.
- Reach out to [support@archermoney.com](mailto:support@archermoney.com) if a deposit stays in **In review** for more than one business day.
