Teammates need an invite from an existing owner or admin before they can sign in to Archer. This guide walks through sending invites, picking the right role, and managing active and pending members.

## Prerequisites

- A role of `owner` or `admin`.
- The email addresses of the teammates you want to invite.

## Send an invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Go to **Settings → Team**. You'll see two sections: **Members** (active accounts) and **Pending invites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite member">
    Enter the teammate's **email** and choose a **role**:

    - `owner` — full workspace control. Reserve for people who run the program.
    - `admin` — same operational access as owner, including agent and Slack management.
    - `approver` — can approve or decline pending orders, but cannot edit agents or settings.
    - `member` — read-only access to agents, activity, team, and their own notification preferences.

    See [Team members](/concepts/team-members) for the full permission matrix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send invite**. Archer creates a pending user row with the email and role you chose, and emails the teammate a sign-in link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for acceptance">
    Once the invitee follows the link and signs in, their pending row is promoted to an active member. They appear in the **Members** list and the pending row disappears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage pending invites

From the **Pending invites** section you can:

- **Resend** — generate a fresh sign-in link if the original was lost or expired.
- **Change role** — update the role on a pending invite before it is accepted.
- **Cancel** — remove the pending row. The sign-in link stops working immediately.

## Manage active members

From the **Members** section you can:

- **Change role** — promote or demote a member. The change takes effect on their next request.
- **Remove** — revoke access immediately. The member is signed out and must be re-invited to return.

## Tips

- Keep at least two `owner` accounts so no single person can lock the workspace by leaving.
- Use `approver` for finance or ops contacts who only need to release pending orders.
- Use `member` for stakeholders who want visibility into Activity without the ability to change anything.
