Before Archer can send a payout to someone, that person must be invited, complete onboarding, and be activated by you. Activation registers them as an approved recipient — without this step, Archer's payments engine will not accept transfers to them. This guide walks through the full lifecycle from first invite to active recipient.

## Invite and activate a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team Members">
    Select **Team Members** from the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the recipient's email">
    Type the recipient's email address into the **Email address** field in the invite form at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. Archer sends the recipient an onboarding email with instructions to create their account and register their payout details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the Invited status">
    The member appears in the team members table with a status of **Invited**. They have received the email but have not yet completed sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the Onboarded status">
    Once the recipient completes the onboarding flow and registers their payout details, their status changes to **Onboarded**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the member">
    Click **Activate** next to the member's name. This adds them to the approved-recipient allowlist for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Active status">
    Once the change confirms, the member's status changes to **Active**. They can now receive payouts from your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk import via CSV

If you need to add a large group of recipients at once, use the CSV import feature instead of inviting one by one.

1. Click **Import CSV** in the page header.
2. In the import modal, upload a CSV file that contains a `name` column and an account identifier column for each person. An `email` column is optional.
3. Archer processes the file and adds each row as an active team member immediately. Imported members appear in the table with status **Active** and are ready to receive payouts.

## Archive a member

To remove a member's access, click the archive icon in their table row. If the member was **Active**, this also removes them from the approved-recipient allowlist — they will no longer be able to receive payouts.

Archived members are hidden from the main table but can be found and restored by clicking **Archived Members** in the page header.

<Warning>
  Only activate members whose payout details have been verified during the Archer onboarding flow. Activating a recipient you have not verified could result in funds being sent to the wrong destination.
</Warning>
