---
title: "Invite workers to claim their Archer account"
description: "Send an invite link from the Workers page so a worker can sign in, claim their own self-custodied wallet, and see rewards land in a recipient dashboard."
---

Adding a worker on the **Workers** page creates a record Archer can send payouts to. Inviting that worker to **claim** their account lets them sign in, take custody of their own wallet, and see their balance and activity in a dedicated recipient dashboard.

Once a worker claims their invite, they sign in with their own email. Archer never signs on their behalf — the wallet is bound to the worker's login, not to your organization.

## Prerequisites

- An Archer account with the **Owner** or **Admin** role.
- The worker already exists on the **Workers** page with a valid email address.

## Send an invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workers page">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **Workers**. Each row in the roster has an invite action on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Next to the worker you want to invite, click **Invite**. Archer generates a one-time claim link and shows it inline with a copy button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Copy the link and send it to the worker over your usual channel — email, Slack DM, whatever they'll see. The link is tied to that worker's email, so only they can complete the claim.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The worker's invite status shows next to their row:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **No badge** | No invite has been issued yet, or a previous invite was revoked. |
| **Pending** | An invite link is live and waiting to be claimed. |
| **Claimed** | The worker has signed in and their wallet is provisioned. |

## Regenerate or revoke a link

If a worker loses their invite link or you sent it to the wrong place:

- Click **Regenerate link** on a pending invite to mint a fresh link. The old link stops working immediately.
- Click **Revoke** on a pending invite to cancel it entirely. You can issue a new invite later from the same row.

Claimed invites can't be regenerated or revoked — the worker's account is already active.

## What the worker sees

When the worker opens the claim link, Archer walks them through a short flow:

1. **Sign in** with the email the invite was sent to. If they sign in with a different email, Archer refuses the claim and asks them to switch accounts.
2. **Wallet setup** happens automatically in the background. Archer provisions a self-custodied wallet bound to the worker's own login.
3. **Recipient dashboard** at `/recipient` opens once setup finishes. The worker sees:
   - Their **USDC on Base** wallet address, with a copy button, for anyone who wants to send them funds.
   - Their **current balance**.
   - **Activity** — recent transactions on their wallet.
   - A **Cash out to your bank** card noting that bank payouts are coming soon.

The worker keeps custody of the wallet. Your organization can send them payouts through agents, but only the worker can sign transactions from their wallet.

## Tips

- Invite links are tied to the email on the worker's record. Fix a typo on the Workers page **before** sending an invite, otherwise the worker will hit an "Wrong account" error.
- Regenerate a link any time you're unsure whether the previous one leaked — old links stop working the moment a new one is issued.
- Inviting a worker to claim is different from [inviting a teammate](/guides/invite-team). Teammates get dashboard access; workers get a recipient view scoped to their own wallet.
