---
title: "Customize reward branding"
description: "Customize each agent's reward email subject, body copy, primary brand color, claim form logo, and Powered by Archer attribution from the agent detail page."
---

Every agent has its own branding for the emails and claim forms it sends. You can edit this on the agent detail page, under **Email & form branding**.

## What you can customize

| Field | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Email title** | Subject line of the reward email. Defaults to "You've got a reward waiting". |
| **Email body** | Body of the reward email. Supports placeholders like `{Recipient}` and `{Company}`. |
| **Primary color** | Accent color used on the email button and on the claim form. |
| **Form logo** | Logo shown at the top of the claim form. PNG or SVG, transparent background recommended. |
| **Remove "Powered by Archer"** | Hides the Archer attribution from emails and forms. Available on paid plans. |

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    From the Agents page, click into the agent you want to brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Email & form branding">
    Scroll to the **Email & form branding** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    Update the title, body, color, and logo. Use the **Preview email** and **Preview form** buttons to see your changes before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the bottom of the page. The branding applies to the next reward this agent fires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    Use **Simulate agent** to send a real test email to yourself and confirm the branding renders as expected across devices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Placeholders

The email body supports a few placeholders that Archer substitutes per recipient:

- `{Recipient}` — the recipient's first name (or email if no name is on file).
- `{Company}` — your workspace's **Sender display name** from Settings.

Anything else in `{ }` is rendered as-is.

## Workspace defaults

A few branding details are set once at the workspace level, not per agent:

- **Sender display name** — shown as the "From" line on every reward email. Edit it in **Settings → General**.
- **Reply-to email** — where recipient replies land. Also under **Settings → General**.
- **Business address** — used for fulfillment and shown in compliance footers. Under **Settings → Business address**.

Per-agent branding overrides workspace defaults where they overlap.

## Tips

- Use a square or horizontal logo. Tall logos get squished in the header.
- Keep the email body under ~120 words. Recipients skim.
- Test in both light and dark mode email clients — the primary color is used as both background and accent in different places.
