---
title: "Invite your team"
description: "Invite teammates to your Archer workspace from Settings, assign Owner, Admin, Approver, or Member roles, and control who can manage campaigns or approvals."
---

Archer workspaces support multiple users with role-based access. Invite teammates from **Settings → Team**.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus account-level destructive actions like deleting the workspace. |
| **Admin** | Create, edit, and archive campaigns; install Slack; manage team; approve and ship Office Orders; edit workspace settings. |
| **Approver** | Approve, confirm, cancel, and ship Office Orders. Read campaigns and Activity. |
| **Member** | Read-only access to campaigns, Activity, Approvals, Orders, and the team list. |

## Send an invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Team">
    From the dashboard sidebar, click **Settings**, then scroll to **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click **Invite**. A modal opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email and role">
    Type the invitee's work email and pick a role from the dropdown. Click **Send invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invitee accepts">
    The invitee receives a Slack-style email link. They sign in through Auth0 (email, Google, or Microsoft) with the **same verified email** the invite was sent to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The role activates as soon as the invitee signs in. Until then, the invite shows as **Pending** in the team list.

## Change a role

From **Settings → Team**, click the role badge next to a user's name. Pick the new role and confirm. Owners cannot demote themselves — ask another owner to do it, or transfer ownership first.

## Remove a teammate

Click the user's row in **Settings → Team** and choose **Remove**. The user loses access immediately. Campaigns they created are unaffected — campaigns are owned by the workspace, not by individuals.

## Tips

- Use **Approver** for finance and ops folks who should sign off on Office Orders but shouldn't be creating or editing campaigns.
- Keep at least two **Owners** at all times so no single person becomes a bottleneck.
- Invite teammates with their work email (the one they sign in to Google or Microsoft with). Auth0 will match the verified identity automatically.
