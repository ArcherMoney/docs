title: "Verify your business to move money"
description: "Complete Archer's one-time business verification to unlock ACH and wallet transfers, and learn what each verification status means."
---

Archer verifies every business before it can move money between a bank account and a wallet. Verification is a one-time step handled by our partner, Bridge. You open a verification link, submit your business details and documents on Bridge's hosted page, and Archer flips your workspace to **Verified** once Bridge approves you.

Until your workspace is verified, ACH payouts and wallet transfers are disabled. Everything else — creating agents, drafting rewards, inviting teammates — works as normal.

## Prerequisites

- **Owner** or **Admin** role. Approvers and Members can't start verification.
- Your **business legal name** filled in during workspace setup. You can update it any time from the profile step in Smart Session setup.
- A verified email on your Archer account. This is the email Bridge uses to send approval updates.

## Start verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    From the dashboard sidebar, click **Settings**. The **Verification** card sits near the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify to move money">
    A modal opens with a summary of what Bridge collects and a **Start verification** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Bridge's hosted flow">
    Verification opens in a new tab. Submit your business details, ownership information, and any requested documents on Bridge's page. Finish there, then come back to Archer — the Settings page updates automatically once you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Most verifications clear in a few minutes. Manual reviews can take until the next business day. Archer emails you when your status changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verification statuses

The **Verification** card on Settings shows a status pill that reflects where Bridge is with your submission.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not started** | You haven't started verification yet. | Click **Verify to move money** to open the flow. |
| **In progress** | You started verification but haven't submitted everything Bridge needs. | Reopen the modal and click **Continue verification** to finish. |
| **In review** | Bridge received your submission and is reviewing it. | Nothing — you'll get an email once review completes. You can close the tab. |
| **Verified** | Bridge approved your business. Money movement is unlocked. | Nothing. Move money as normal. |
| **Rejected** | Bridge couldn't verify your business with what you submitted. | Review the reason shown in the modal, correct the details, and click **Try again**. |
| **Paused** | Bridge paused your verification and needs to talk to you. | Contact [support](mailto:support@archermoney.com). |
| **Unavailable** | This account type can no longer be verified through Archer. | Contact [support](mailto:support@archermoney.com). |

## If verification fails to start

The modal surfaces a short error if Archer can't hand you off to Bridge. Common causes:

- **Missing business name** — add your business legal name in your profile, then reopen the modal.
- **Missing email** — add and verify an email on your Archer account, then reopen the modal.
- **Account type not supported** — contact [support](mailto:support@archermoney.com); only certain account types can be verified through Bridge.

## After you're verified

Once your workspace flips to **Verified**:

- ACH payroll and partner payouts run without extra prompts.
- Wallet-to-bank and bank-to-wallet transfers work from any agent that needs them.
- The **Verification** card stays on Settings for reference but the **Verify to move money** button disappears.

You don't need to re-verify unless Bridge later pauses or offboards your account. If that happens, the status pill on Settings tells you what to do next.
