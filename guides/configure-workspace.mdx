Your workspace settings control what recipients see on every reward email and what address Archer uses on shipping confirmations. This guide walks through filling out **Settings → General**.

## Prerequisites

- A role of `owner` or `admin`. Other roles can read these fields but cannot change them.

## Fill in the fields

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    From the dashboard, go to **Settings → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set workspace name">
    Enter the name your team uses internally — for example, `Northwind Co.` Archer uses this on the dashboard and in admin emails. It is not surfaced to reward recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sender display name">
    This is the name recipients see as the sender on every reward email. Use something personal and recognizable like `Vivek at Northwind` rather than a generic company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set reply-to email">
    Enter the inbox you want recipient replies to land in — for example, `hello@northwind.io`. Reward emails set this as the `Reply-To` header so recipients who hit reply reach a human, not a no-reply mailbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit business address">
    Click **Edit** next to **Business address** and fill in the modal:

    - **Address line 1** (required)
    - **Address line 2** (optional — apartment, suite, or floor)
    - **City** (required)
    - **State** (required)
    - **Postal code** (required)
    - **Country** (required)

    Click **Save address**. Archer stores the structured address and uses it on shipping confirmations for orders you place.

    The Save button stays disabled until every required field has content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When recipients see each field

| Field | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Workspace name | Dashboard chrome, admin emails. |
| Sender display name | `From` line on every reward email. |
| Reply-to email | `Reply-To` header on every reward email. |
| Business address | Shipping confirmations and order summaries. |
