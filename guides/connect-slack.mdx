The Integrations page is where you connect Archer to the other tools your team already uses. The first integration available is Slack — once your workspace is connected, anyone in it can launch the order and referral flows with a slash command instead of opening the dashboard.

## When to use this

Use the Slack integration if your team kicks off payouts or referrals from chat, or if you want a single place to manage workspace-level connections for your Archer account.

## Connect your Slack workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Select **Integrations** from the left sidebar of the Archer dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the install">
    Under the **Slack** section, click **Add to Slack**. Archer redirects you to Slack's authorization screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the requested scopes">
    Slack shows the scopes the Archer app requests — `chat:write`, `app_mentions:read`, `channels:join`, and `commands`. Select the workspace you want to connect and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Slack redirects you back to Archer. The Slack section now shows your workspace ID with a green **Connected** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only one Slack workspace can be linked to an Archer account at a time. To swap workspaces, disconnect the current one first.

## Use the slash commands

Once your workspace is connected, every member of that Slack workspace can run these commands in any channel or DM where the Archer app is installed:

| Command  | What it does                                                          |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/order` | Opens a modal to create a new payout from your Archer account.        |
| `/refer` | Opens a modal to send a referral to a new recipient.                  |

Submitting either modal runs the action against the Archer account that installed the workspace, using the same paying limits and team member rules as the dashboard.

```text Example
/order
```

If the command returns the message **"Slack Workspace not found. Open the Archer app, click Integrations on the left, and connect your Slack workspace."**, the workspace isn't linked yet — connect it from the Integrations page and try again.

## Disconnect your Slack workspace

To remove the integration, open **Integrations**, find the **Slack** section, and click **Delete** next to the connected workspace. Archer revokes the bot token and the slash commands stop working immediately. You can reconnect at any time by clicking **Add to Slack** again.
