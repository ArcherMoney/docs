---
title: "Connect Slack"
description: "Install the Archer Slack app, connect a workspace, create a Slack-triggered agent, and fire rewards from any channel with the /archer slash command."
---

Connecting Slack lets teammates fire an agent by typing `/archer` in any channel. It's the right fit for office orders and other ad-hoc requests that don't come from a programmatic source.

## Prerequisites

- A Slack workspace where you can install apps.
- The **Owner** or **Admin** role on your Archer account.

## 1. Install the Archer Slack app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Slack">
    On the Slack card, click **Add**. Slack's OAuth screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace and approve">
    Choose the workspace you want to connect, review the requested scopes, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Slack redirects you back to Archer. The Slack card now shows a green **Connected** badge and a link to your workspace URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Create a Slack-triggered agent

Slack agents work just like webhook agents, except their trigger is set to **Slack**. You can only have one active Slack agent at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agents → New agent">
    Click **New agent** on the Agents page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category">
    Office Order is the most common choice for Slack agents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the logic">
    For example: "When a teammate runs `/archer` with a recipient and item, ship the item to their address."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set trigger to Slack and save">
    Save the agent. If a Slack agent already exists, Archer will tell you to pause or archive the existing one first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Fire the slash command

In any channel in the connected workspace, type:

```
/archer Send a YETI tumbler to jordan@acme.com
```

Archer routes the command to your Slack agent. If the agent is an Office Order, it lands in the **Approvals** queue waiting on a sign-off. Customer Gift and Partner Gift Slack agents send immediately.

## Removing or reconnecting

From **Integrations**, on the Slack card:

- **Remove** — disconnects the workspace from Archer. Your Slack agent will stop firing until you add Slack again.
- To refresh the OAuth token (for example, after a Slack workspace owner change or a permissions update), click **Remove**, then **Add** to reinstall.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely fix |
|---|---|
| `/archer` does nothing | Confirm the Slack agent is **Active**, not paused or archived. |
| "An active Slack agent already exists" | Pause or archive the existing Slack agent before creating a new one. |
| Slash command runs but the wrong reward sends | Check the agent's **Logic** field. The phrasing is what Archer matches against. |
| The command suddenly stops working | Remove Slack from **Integrations** and click **Add** to reinstall. The OAuth token may have rotated. |
| "Something went wrong connecting Slack" toast | Try **Add** again. If it persists, confirm your Archer account has the **Owner** or **Admin** role. |
