---
title: "Connect Slack"
description: "Install the Archer Slack app, connect a workspace, create a Slack-triggered campaign, and fire rewards from any channel with the /archer slash command."
---

Connecting Slack lets teammates fire a campaign by typing `/archer` in any channel. It's the right fit for office orders and other ad-hoc requests that don't come from a programmatic source.

## Prerequisites

- A Slack workspace where you can install apps.
- The **Owner** or **Admin** role on your Archer account.

## 1. Install the Archer Slack app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Slack">
    Click **Install Slack**. Slack's OAuth screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace and approve">
    Choose the workspace you want to connect, review the requested scopes, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Slack redirects you back to Archer. The Integrations page now shows **Connected to \{Workspace Name\}** with a **Reinstall** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Create a Slack-triggered campaign

Slack campaigns work just like webhook campaigns, except their trigger is set to **Slack**. You can only have one active Slack campaign at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns → Create campaign">
    Click **Create campaign** on the Campaigns page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category">
    Office Order is the most common choice for Slack campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the logic">
    For example: "When a teammate runs `/archer` with a recipient and item, ship the item to their address."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set trigger to Slack and save">
    Save the campaign. If a Slack campaign already exists, Archer will tell you to pause or archive the existing one first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Fire the slash command

In any channel in the connected workspace, type:

```
/archer Send a YETI tumbler to jordan@acme.com
```

Archer routes the command to your Slack campaign. If the campaign is an Office Order, it lands in the **Approvals** queue waiting on a sign-off. Customer Gift and Partner Gift Slack campaigns send immediately.

## Reinstalling or disconnecting

From **Integrations**:

- **Reinstall Slack** — refreshes the OAuth token. Use this after a Slack workspace owner change or a permissions update.
- **Disconnect** — removes the Slack integration. Your Slack campaign will stop firing until you reinstall.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely fix |
|---|---|
| `/archer` does nothing | Confirm the Slack campaign is **Active**, not paused or archived. |
| "An active Slack campaign already exists" | Pause or archive the existing Slack campaign before creating a new one. |
| Slash command runs but the wrong reward sends | Check the campaign's **Logic** field. The phrasing is what Archer matches against. |
| The command suddenly stops working | Reinstall Slack from **Integrations**. The OAuth token may have rotated. |
