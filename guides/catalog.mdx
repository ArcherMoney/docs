The **Catalog** page in the dashboard lists the items currently available to your account as rewards. Use it to see what your agents can send before you write or edit their logic.

## When to use the catalog

- **Before creating an agent** — confirm the items you want to reward with are already linked to your account.
- **While writing agent logic** — copy item names and customization options into your agent's plain-language logic so it picks the right reward.
- **For approvals** — preview what an Office Order will ship before approving it.

## What you'll see

Each catalog item shows:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Item title. If a vendor link is set, the name links out in a new tab. |
| **Image** | Product image, when available. Items without an image show a placeholder. |
| **Price** | USD price, when set. Items without a price are still sendable — pricing is finalized at fulfillment. |
| **Customization options** | Key/value pairs the agent can fill in when sending (for example, `Size: M` or `Color: Navy`). |

If no items are linked to your account yet, the page shows an empty state. Reach out to your Archer contact to have items added.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Catalog">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **Catalog**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the grid">
    Each card is an item linked to your account. Click an item name to open the vendor page in a new tab when a link is set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reference items from an agent">
    Note the item name and any customization keys you want to use. In your agent's [Logic field](/guides/create-an-agent#4-write-the-logic), describe the item the same way it appears here — for example, "send a Navy Medium branded hoodie".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can see the catalog

Every member of your workspace can view the Catalog. The same items appear for everyone on the account — there's no per-user filtering. Items are linked to accounts by the Archer team, not self-serve from the dashboard.

## FAQ

**Can I add items myself?**
Not from the dashboard. Items are added and linked to your account by Archer. If you need a new item or vendor added, contact your Archer point of contact.

**Why don't I see prices on some items?**
Price is optional. When it's missing, Archer settles the final cost at fulfillment.

**Do items show up immediately after they're linked?**
Yes — reload the page and the new items appear right away.
