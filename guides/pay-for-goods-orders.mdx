---
title: "Pay for goods orders with credits"
description: "Add a saved payment method, accept direct-charge consent, and prepay for Office Order fulfillment with credits that Archer draws down as orders are approved."
---

Office Orders that ship physical goods need a way to pay for fulfillment. Archer settles approved goods orders against a prepaid **credit balance** first, then falls back to your saved payment method for any remainder. This guide walks through getting your workspace ready and topping up credits.

## How payment works

When an Office Order is approved, Archer charges in this order:

1. **Credit balance** — if you have one, Archer deducts the item and shipping cost from it before charging anything else.
2. **Saved payment method** — covers the remainder, or the full amount if your balance is zero.

You always see the split on the order: **Item** + **Shipping** = total charged.

## Get your workspace ready

Both credits and direct charges require a one-time setup on the **Plan** tab under **Settings**. Only **Owners** and **Admins** can complete it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a saved payment method">
    On the Plan card, click **Set up payment method**. Archer redirects you to a hosted Stripe page to save a card. When you return, the **Direct goods charges** tile updates to show the card on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept direct-charge consent">
    The Plan card shows a consent block under **Direct goods charges**. Read it, then click **Accept consent**. This authorizes Archer to charge the saved card for approved goods orders and credit deposits.

    Consent is tied to the specific saved payment method. If you swap cards later, you'll be asked to re-accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm readiness">
    When the **Direct goods charges** badge reads **Ready**, the workspace can charge for goods orders and you can start buying credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy credits

Once the workspace is ready, the **Credits** tab appears under **Settings** and a balance pill shows in the top bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Credits">
    The Credits card shows your current balance, recent activity, and a deposit form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a deposit amount">
    Type a USD amount and click **Buy credits**. Archer charges your saved card and adds the credits to the balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle authentication if asked">
    If your bank requires 3-D Secure or similar, Archer prompts you to authenticate in-line. The balance updates within a few seconds of confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deposits show up in **Recent activity** alongside debits for fulfilled orders so you can reconcile spend.

## Where this shows up

| Surface | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Top bar** | A credit-balance pill next to your workspace switcher. Click it to jump to the Credits tab. |
| **Approvals queue** | Each Office Order shows **Item** and **Shipping** as separate lines so you can preview the full charge before approving. |
| **Orders page** | Fulfilled orders show the same **Item · Shipping** breakdown. |
| **Catalog** | Item prices show **excl. shipping** — shipping is quoted at approval time based on the destination. |

## When to use credits vs. card only

- **Use credits** if you want predictable monthly spend, prepaid billing for finance, or a single invoice per top-up instead of one charge per order.
- **Skip credits** if you'd rather see each goods order as its own card transaction. The setup is the same — just don't deposit a balance.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Setup needed" on the Credits or Plan card** — You're missing a saved payment method, direct-charge consent, or both. Re-run the steps above on the Plan tab.
- **Deposit failed** — Stripe rejected the charge. Check the card in **Manage billing** (Customer Portal) or add a new payment method.
- **Order approved but not fulfilling** — If your balance can't cover the order and the saved card fails, the order pauses until you resolve the payment. Top up credits or fix the card to retry.
