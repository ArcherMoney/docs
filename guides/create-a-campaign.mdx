---
title: "Create a campaign"
description: "Walk through creating a campaign in the Archer dashboard — naming it, choosing a category, writing the logic, picking a trigger, and sending a test."
---

This guide shows how to create a campaign end-to-end from the dashboard. By the end you'll have an active campaign with branding configured and a working test gift.

<Note>
  Campaigns were previously called **agents**. If you see "Agent" in older screenshots, it's the same primitive.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- An Archer account.
- The **Owner** or **Admin** role. Members and Approvers can read campaigns but can't create or edit them.

## 1. Open the Campaigns page

In the dashboard sidebar, click **Campaigns**. Click **Create campaign** in the top right. The editor opens with the **Settings** tab selected.

## 2. Pick a category

Pick one of:

- **Office Order** — for internal swag and supplies. Runs through the Approvals and Orders queues.
- **Customer Gift** — for end users. Sends immediately.
- **Partner Gift** — for partners and affiliates. Sends immediately.

See [Categories](/concepts/categories) for fulfillment differences.

## 3. Name the campaign

Give it a short, internal label like "Onboarding gift" or "Q3 affiliate payout". This is what you and your team see in Campaigns, Activity, and Approvals.

<Note>
  Slack-triggered campaigns always show as **Slack Campaign** in lists regardless of the name you set. You can only have one Slack campaign per workspace.
</Note>

## 4. Write the logic

The **Logic** field is the most important thing on the page. It's a plain-language description Archer uses to match incoming events. Two-clause sentences work best:

- ✅ "When a referred user finishes onboarding, send the referrer a $25 Amazon gift card."
- ✅ "When a teammate runs `/archer` with a recipient and item, ship the item to their address."
- ❌ "Reward partners."

Be specific about the trigger condition, who receives the reward, and what the reward is.

## 5. Pick a trigger

Choose one of:

- **Webhook** — your product or backend `POST`s events to a URL Archer assigns.
- **Slack** — a teammate runs `/archer` in your connected Slack workspace.
- **Upload** — Archer ingests a recurring CSV you provide.
- **Scraper** — Archer crawls a third-party source on your behalf.

Slack requires the Archer Slack app to be installed — see [Connect Slack](/guides/connect-slack). See [Triggers](/concepts/triggers) for when to pick which.

## 6. Save

Click **Save**. The campaign is created as **Active**. The detail page now shows the campaign's:

- **Webhook URL** (for webhook campaigns) — copy this into your producer.
- **Email and form branding** — customize how recipients see the reward.
- **Recent activity** — empty for now.

## 7. Send a test gift

Scroll to **Simulate campaign** on the detail page. Enter your own email under **Recipient email** and click **Send test →**. Archer sends a real reward email so you can preview exactly what recipients will receive.

## 8. Wire up the real trigger

For webhook campaigns, hand the **Webhook URL** to whoever is integrating your product or backend. They'll find request format and idempotency rules in [Sending events](/developers/sending-events).

For Slack campaigns, run the slash command in any channel of your connected workspace.

## Editing a campaign later

Open the campaign from the Campaigns page and use the **Settings** tab to update name, category, logic, status, and branding. The webhook URL doesn't change when you edit other fields.

## Pausing or archiving

- **Pause** — flip the "Campaign is active" toggle on the Settings tab. Webhook events keep arriving but no rewards send.
- **Archive** — delete the campaign. The webhook URL stops producing rewards. Archived campaigns can't be restored; create a new one if you need the same rule back.
