---

Workers who claim an Archer invite land in a recipient dashboard where they can cash USDC out of their wallet to a local bank. Payouts settle in local currency over instant rails — **SPEI** in Mexico, **Pix** in Brazil, and **Bre-B** in Colombia — after a one-time identity check.

## Prerequisites

- You accepted an invite from a workspace and finished claiming your account. If you haven't, follow the claim link the workspace sent you and sign in.
- Your Archer wallet holds at least $1 in USDC on Base.
- You have a bank account in Mexico, Brazil, or Colombia, and the account holder is you.

## Verify your identity

Cash-out is gated behind a one-time identity check.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cash out">
    Sign in to your recipient dashboard. In the **Cash out** panel, click **Verify to cash out**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete verification">
    A verification window opens. Follow the prompts to submit your ID and personal details, then close the window when you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Approval is usually instant. The **Cash out** panel unlocks as soon as your status is **Approved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Verification is tied to the **payout currency** you pick. If you later add a bank in a different country, you may be asked to complete a short follow-up review for that region.
</Tip>

## Add a bank account

Add the bank account you want your cash-out to land in. Each currency uses a different local rail, so the form fields change based on what you pick.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a payout currency">
    In the **Cash out** panel, choose **MXN**, **BRL**, or **COP**. This selects the rail used for the transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the bank details">
    Enter the account details for the currency you picked:

    | Currency | Rail | Required details |
    |---|---|---|
    | **MXN** | SPEI | 18-digit **CLABE** |
    | **BRL** | Pix | **Pix key** and **CPF or CNPJ** |
    | **COP** | Bre-B | **Bre-B key** |

    Also enter your **first name**, **last name**, and full **beneficiary address** (street, city, state, postal code, country). The account holder must be you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the account">
    Click **Add bank account**. The account appears in your bank list and is selected automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add multiple accounts and switch between them at cash-out time. You can add accounts in different currencies from the same dashboard.

## Cash out USDC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a bank account">
    Select one of your saved bank accounts in the **Cash out** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Type an amount in USDC. The minimum is **$1**, and amounts are settled to the whole cent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and sign">
    Click **Cash out to bank** and approve the wallet signature. Archer moves your USDC on Base to the cash-out address, converts it to local currency, and sends it to your bank.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Typical settlement times:

- **SPEI (MXN):** minutes to a few hours
- **Pix (BRL):** minutes
- **Bre-B (COP):** minutes to a few hours

## Track a cash-out

Every cash-out shows up under **Recent cash-outs** on your dashboard. Statuses update live while a payout is in flight:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sending** | Your USDC is on its way to the cash-out address. |
| **Received** | Archer received your USDC and is converting it to local currency. |
| **In review** | The payout is under manual review. You'll get an email when it clears. |
| **Sending to bank** | The local-currency transfer is on its way to your bank. |
| **Completed** | The payout settled to your bank account. |
| **Returning** / **Returned** | Your bank couldn't accept the payout — the USDC is being returned to your wallet. |
| **Needs attention** / **Failed** | Something blocked the payout. Contact support. |

## Troubleshooting

- **"Finish verifying your identity before cashing out to a bank."** — Your identity check hasn't been approved yet. Re-open the verification window and finish it.
- **"Verification for this payout currency isn't complete yet."** — You added a bank in a new region and need to complete a short follow-up review. Open the verification window again to finish it.
- **"Insufficient USDC in your smart wallet. Top up and try again."** — Your wallet balance is below the amount you entered. Ask the workspace that pays you to top you up, then retry.
- **"Cash-out cancelled."** — You dismissed the signature prompt in your wallet. Click **Cash out to bank** again and approve the signature.
- **Payout stuck in "Returning."** — Double-check the bank details you saved (CLABE, Pix key, or Bre-B key). Once the funds are back in your wallet, add a corrected account and retry.

For anything else, reach out to your workspace admin or Archer support.
