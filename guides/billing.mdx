title: "Manage billing and plans"
description: "Upgrade your Archer plan, start a subscription with Stripe Checkout, and manage payment methods or invoices from the Stripe customer portal."
---

Archer subscriptions are billed through Stripe. You start or change a paid plan from **Settings → Plan** in the dashboard, then manage payment methods, invoices, and cancellation in a Stripe-hosted customer portal.

## Plans

| Plan | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | Small teams getting started with one or two agents. |
| **Pro** | Growing teams that need more agents, approvers, and branding controls. |
| **Enterprise** | Larger orgs with custom volume, security, or contractual needs. |

Every workspace starts on **Basic**. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Who can manage billing

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner**, **Admin** | View the current plan, start checkout, open the Stripe portal, change plans, update payment methods, cancel. |
| **Approver**, **Member** | View the current plan only. |

If you don't see the **Upgrade** or **Manage plan** button, ask an owner or admin on your workspace.

## Start a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    From the dashboard sidebar, click **Settings**, then scroll to **Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Click **Upgrade** on **Pro** or **Enterprise**. Archer opens a Stripe Checkout page in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Fill in your billing email, card, and (if required) tax ID. Stripe handles the form — Archer never sees the card number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Subscribe**. Stripe redirects you back to **Settings → Plan** with the new plan active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each workspace is one Stripe customer. If you start checkout twice for the same plan, Archer reuses the existing customer and avoids creating duplicates.

## Manage an existing subscription

Once you have an active subscription, **Settings → Plan** shows a **Manage plan** button instead of **Upgrade**. Click it to open the **Stripe customer portal** in a new tab. From the portal you can:

- Update the card on file
- Change your billing email or address
- Download invoices and receipts
- Switch between Basic, Pro, and Enterprise
- Cancel at the end of the current period

Changes you make in the portal sync back to the dashboard within a minute.

## What the dashboard shows

The **Plan** card on **Settings** displays:

- **Current tier** — Basic, Pro, or Enterprise.
- **Status** — `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, or `canceled`.
- **Renewal date** — the end of the current billing period, when the plan renews or cancels.
- **Payment method** — card brand and last four digits, when one is on file.

If the status is **past_due**, open the customer portal and update the card to restore service. Archer keeps the workspace on the paid plan during a short grace period before downgrading.

## Cancel

Open the **Manage plan** portal and click **Cancel plan**. The workspace stays on the paid plan until the renewal date, then drops back to **Basic**. You can resubscribe at any time from **Settings → Plan**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Billing is not configured"** — the workspace's Stripe environment isn't set up. Contact support@archermoney.com.
- **"Billing customer not found"** — you tried to open the portal before completing a checkout. Start a subscription first, or contact support if you previously had one.
- **Checkout opens in the same tab and you can't get back** — use your browser back button. Your account isn't charged unless Stripe confirms the subscription.
