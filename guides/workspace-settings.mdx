The **Settings** page in the dashboard is where workspace-wide configuration lives. Most fields here affect every agent and every reward — set them once when you start, then revisit only when something changes.

## General

| Field | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Workspace name** | Internal label shown in the top bar and on shared resources. |
| **Sender display name** | What recipients see as the "From" line on every reward email. |
| **Reply-to email** | Where recipient replies go. Usually a shared inbox like `rewards@yourcompany.com`. |

Per-agent branding (subject line, body, color, logo) overrides workspace defaults where they overlap. See [Customize reward branding](/guides/customize-rewards).

## Business address

Used for fulfillment and shown in compliance footers on outbound emails. Fill in:

- Address line 1 and 2
- City, state, postal code, country

Click **Edit** under the address card to open the editor.

## Notifications

Workspace-wide notification preferences. You can opt in or out of:

- **Approval requests** — email a digest when new Office Orders land in the queue.
- **Reward delivery** — email when a reward is successfully delivered.
- **Failures and bounces** — email if a reward email bounces or a fulfillment fails.

Notifications are workspace-level, not per-user. Adjust them based on who actively monitors the dashboard.

## Plan

The **Plan** tab shows your current subscription, billing period, and payment method. Owners and admins can start a paid subscription or manage an existing one without leaving the dashboard.

### Start a subscription

If your account does not yet have a paid subscription, the Plan tab shows a tier picker and a **Start subscription** button:

1. Pick a tier — **Basic**, **Pro**, or **Enterprise**.
2. Click **Start *tier* subscription**.
3. Archer redirects you to a Stripe-hosted Checkout page to enter payment details.
4. After Checkout, Stripe returns you to Settings. Archer refreshes the page once the subscription is provisioned.

If you close Checkout without completing payment, your plan is unchanged. You can retry from the same Plan tab.

### Manage an existing subscription

Once a subscription is active, the Plan tab shows:

- **Tier** and **status** (Active, Trialing, Past due, Canceled, etc.).
- **Billing period** — the next renewal date, or the cancellation date if the subscription is set to cancel.
- **Payment method** — the brand and last four digits of the card on file.

Click **Open Customer Portal** to update the card, download invoices, change tier, or cancel. The Customer Portal is hosted by Stripe and opens in the same window. Archer reflects any changes you make on the next page load.

### Who can change billing

- **Owners** and **Admins** can start a subscription and open the Customer Portal.
- **Approvers** and **Members** see the Plan tab as read-only.

### Branding entitlements

Some branding options — like hiding "Powered by Archer" — remain gated to paid tiers. See [Customize reward branding](/guides/customize-rewards).

## Who can edit Settings

- **Owners** and **Admins** can edit every section.
- **Approvers** and **Members** have read-only access.
