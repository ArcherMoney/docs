The **Settings** page in the dashboard is where workspace-wide configuration lives. Most fields here affect every agent and every reward — set them once when you start, then revisit only when something changes.

## General

| Field | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Workspace name** | Internal label shown in the top bar and on shared resources. |
| **Sender display name** | What recipients see as the "From" line on every reward email. |
| **Reply-to email** | Where recipient replies go. Usually a shared inbox like `rewards@yourcompany.com`. |

Per-agent branding (subject line, body, color, logo) overrides workspace defaults where they overlap. See [Customize reward branding](/guides/customize-rewards).

## Business address

Used for fulfillment and shown in compliance footers on outbound emails. Fill in:

- Address line 1 and 2
- City, state, postal code, country

Click **Edit** under the address card to open the editor.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** tab controls which emails Archer sends to your workspace. Each toggle is independent — flip one and the change saves immediately.

| Toggle | What you get | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Approval needed** | An email when a reward is waiting on your sign-off in Approvals. | On |
| **Transaction receipts** | An email each time a payment is sent. | Off |
| **Security alerts** | Sign-in and account security notifications. | On |
| **Weekly summary** | A Monday digest of what your campaigns sent last week. | Off |
| **Daily digest** | One daily summary instead of per-event emails. | Off |

In-app notifications are always on and are not affected by these toggles.

Only **Owners** and **Admins** can change notification settings. **Approvers** and **Members** can view the current settings but cannot edit them.

<Tip>
  If you want a quieter inbox, turn on **Daily digest** or **Weekly summary** and turn off **Approval needed** and **Transaction receipts**. You will still see everything in the dashboard.
</Tip>

## Team

Add and manage teammates. See [Invite your team](/guides/invite-team).

## Plan

The **Plan** card shows your current plan and entitlements. Click **Manage plan** to upgrade, downgrade, or update billing. Some branding options (like hiding "Powered by Archer") are gated to paid plans.

## Who can edit Settings

- **Owners** and **Admins** can edit every section.
- **Approvers** and **Members** have read-only access.
