The **Settings** page in the dashboard is where workspace-wide configuration lives. Most fields here affect every agent and every reward — set them once when you start, then revisit only when something changes.

## General

| Field | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Workspace name** | Internal label shown in the top bar and on shared resources. |
| **Sender display name** | What recipients see as the "From" line on every reward email. |
| **Reply-to email** | Where recipient replies go. Usually a shared inbox like `rewards@yourcompany.com`. |

Per-agent branding (subject line, body, color, logo) overrides workspace defaults where they overlap. See [Customize reward branding](/guides/customize-rewards).

## Business address

Used for fulfillment and shown in compliance footers on outbound emails. Fill in:

- Address line 1 and 2
- City, state, postal code, country

Click **Edit** under the address card to open the editor.

## Notifications

Workspace-wide notification preferences. You can opt in or out of:

- **Approval requests** — email a digest when new Office Orders land in the queue.
- **Reward delivery** — email when a reward is successfully delivered.
- **Failures and bounces** — email if a reward email bounces or a fulfillment fails.

Notifications are workspace-level, not per-user. Adjust them based on who actively monitors the dashboard.

## Plan

The **Plan** tab shows your current subscription, billing period, and payment method on file. Use it to start a subscription, manage billing details, or check renewal status.

### Start a subscription

New workspaces sign up for **Archer Pro** at $12,000/year with a 30-day free trial. You will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

1. Open **Settings → Plan**.
2. Click **Start 30-day free trial**.
3. Complete checkout on Stripe's hosted page using your payment details.
4. You're redirected back to the Plan tab, where the badge updates to **Trialing** once the subscription is provisioned.

The Plan tab polls for a few seconds after checkout to pick up the new subscription. If it doesn't appear, click **Refresh status**.

### Manage billing

Once a subscription exists, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe Customer Portal in a new session. Use the portal to:

- Update the card on file
- Download invoices and receipts
- Change billing email and address
- Cancel or resume your subscription

Changes made in the portal sync back to Archer automatically — refresh the Plan tab if you don't see them right away.

### Subscription status

The badge next to your plan name reflects the current Stripe status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trialing** | You're inside the 30-day free trial. No charges yet. |
| **Active** | Subscription is current and paid. |
| **Past due** | A recent payment failed. Open **Manage billing** to update your card. |
| **Canceled** | Subscription has ended. Start a new one to restore access. |
| **Paused** | Billing is paused. Use Manage billing to resume. |

### Who can change the plan

- **Owners** and **Admins** can start checkout and open the Customer Portal.
- **Approvers** and **Members** can view the current plan and status, but cannot make billing changes.

## Who can edit Settings

- **Owners** and **Admins** can edit every section.
- **Approvers** and **Members** have read-only access.
