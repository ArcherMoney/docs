title: "Approve and ship Office Orders"
description: "Approve, confirm, cancel, and ship Office Orders across the Approvals and Orders queues, see which roles can act, and how each decision moves the lifecycle."
---

Office Order campaigns don't send rewards immediately. Every event they receive enters a multi-step lifecycle that flows across two dashboard pages: **Approvals** (sign-offs and address confirmation) and **Orders** (shipping).

For an overview of the statuses involved, see [Activity, approvals & orders](/concepts/activity-and-approvals).

## Who can act

Anyone with the **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Approver** role on the workspace can approve, confirm, cancel, and mark sent. Members can view both queues but can't take action.

See [Invite your team](/guides/invite-team) for how roles are assigned.

## Step 1: Approve in Approvals

Open **Approvals** from the sidebar. Each row in `PENDING_APPROVAL` shows:

- The campaign that fired the order
- The recipient
- The item Archer plans to send
- The quoted price
- When the request was made

Click into a row to see the full event payload and any notes from the requester. From the row you can:

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | Order moves to `PENDING_CLAIM`. Archer emails the recipient a form to collect their shipping address. |
| **Cancel** | Order moves to `CANCELLED` and disappears from the queue. It does not appear in Activity. |

For campaigns that already include a shipping address with the event, orders skip `PENDING_CLAIM` and land in Approvals as `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` instead — see the next step.

## Step 2: Confirm the address

Once the recipient submits their shipping form (or the campaign provides one upfront), the order moves to `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` and reappears in Approvals with a **Confirm address** action.

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Confirm** | Order moves to `PENDING_FULFILLMENT` and shows up on the Orders page. |
| **Cancel** | Order moves to `CANCELLED`. |

Use this step to sanity-check addresses before you ship.

## Step 3: Ship from Orders

Open **Orders** from the sidebar. Each row is a confirmed order ready to fulfill — item, recipient, vendor, price, and shipping address. After you've packed and shipped, click **Mark sent**.

The order moves to `SENT` and shows up as `sent` on the Activity page. Marking sent is irreversible — there's no undo.

## Tips

- Set up campaign **Logic** carefully — clear instructions reduce back-and-forth at approval time.
- Confirm addresses promptly. Orders sit in `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` until someone acts, and recipients won't know there's a delay.
- Use the **Orders** page as your daily shipping worklist. Batch approvals during the day and ship from a single queue at the end.
- For repeating low-cost rewards, consider switching the campaign to a Customer Gift category to skip the Approvals queue entirely (if your governance allows).
