title: "Approve Office Orders"
description: "Review, approve, or decline Office Orders in the Approvals queue, see which roles can act, what each row shows, and how decisions affect fulfillment."
---

Office Order agents don't send rewards immediately. Instead, every event they receive becomes a row in the **Approvals** queue waiting on a sign-off from someone with the right role.

## Who can approve

Anyone with the **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Approver** role on the workspace can act on the queue. Members can view it but can't approve or decline.

See [Invite your team](/guides/invite-team) for how roles are assigned.

## Reviewing the queue

Open **Approvals** from the dashboard sidebar. The page has two tabs, each with a live count of items waiting:

- **Outbound Approvals** — Office Orders waiting on the first sign-off, before Archer reaches out to the recipient for their shipping address.
- **Purchase Approvals** — Office Orders where the recipient has confirmed their address, and Archer needs your final go-ahead to purchase and ship the item.

Switch tabs to see each list. Each row shows:

- The agent that fired the order
- The category (always Office Order for queued items)
- The recipient
- The item Archer plans to send
- The recipient's shipping address (once confirmed, on the Purchase tab)
- When the request was made

## Acting on a request

The action on each row depends on which tab you're in:

| Tab | Primary action | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Outbound Approvals** | **Approve** | The order moves to the Purchase tab. Archer reaches out to the recipient to confirm their shipping address. |
| **Purchase Approvals** | **Confirm** | The order moves to fulfillment. It appears as `delivered` in Activity once shipped or settled. |
| Either tab | **Cancel** | The order is removed from the queue. It does not appear in Activity. |
| Either tab | **Approve all** | Bulk-acts on every order in the current tab. Use carefully — there's no undo. |

Approvals are recorded with the approver's identity for audit purposes.

## Tips

- Set up agent **Logic** carefully — clear instructions reduce the back-and-forth on approvals.
- Use the **Date range** filter in Approvals to focus on recent requests during high-volume periods.
- For repeating low-cost Office Orders, consider switching the agent to a Customer Gift category to skip the queue entirely (if your governance allows).
