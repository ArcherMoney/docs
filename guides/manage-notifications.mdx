Archer's notification preferences let you choose which emails you receive on your own account. Settings are per member — toggling them only affects your inbox, never your teammates'.

## Prerequisites

- A signed-in Archer account in any role.

## Update your preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle each category">
    Each toggle controls one notification category:

    - **Approval needed** — a reward is waiting on your sign-off in [Approvals](/concepts/approvals). Recommended on for `owner`, `admin`, and `approver`.
    - **Weekly summary** — a Monday digest of every reward your agents sent last week.
    - **Daily digest** — one quiet end-of-day summary instead of per-event pings.

    Changes save automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Defaults

A new account starts with **Approval needed** on and the two summaries off, so you get the email that requires action without a stream of recaps. Adjust at any time.

## What each email looks like

| Category | Subject example |
|---|---|
| Approval needed | `Maya Chen has a cake pending approval` |
| Weekly summary | `Northwind Co. — last week: 12 rewards sent` |
| Daily digest | `Northwind Co. — today: 3 rewards sent` |

Every email uses your workspace's **sender display name** and **reply-to email**. If you want to change either, see [Configure your workspace](/guides/configure-workspace).
