Archer maintains a complete audit trail of every transaction executed through your account. Payout events are captured directly from Archer's payments engine and surfaced in the Transaction History view alongside any bank transfer activity. You can search, filter, page through results, and export to CSV for accounting or compliance purposes.

## Accessing transaction history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transaction History">
    Select **Transaction History** in the sidebar. The page loads a chronological list of all transactions associated with your payout account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse and filter">
    Use the controls at the top of the page to narrow results:

    - **Search** — filter by counterparty, reference, or transaction ID
    - **Type filter** — show only payouts, deposits, or withdrawals
    - **Date range** — set a start and end date to scope results to a specific period
  </Step>

  <Step title="View transaction details">
    Click any row to open the details panel. It shows the full transaction ID, counterparty, amount, fee breakdown, rule type, sequence number, and timestamp.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export to CSV">
    Click **Export** in the top-right corner to open the export modal. Select a date range and Archer downloads a CSV file containing all matching transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each record in the list shows: **To/From** counterparty, **status**, **time**, and **amount**.

## Payout events

Payout transactions are recorded by Archer's payments engine. Three event types are captured:

| Event | Description |
|---|---|
| `TransferExecuted` | A standard transfer was sent to a recipient |
| `PayoutExecuted` | A payout was sent to a recipient |
| `CustomCallExecuted` | A custom action was executed |

Every payout record includes the following fields:

- **initiator** — the party that triggered the execution
- **account** — your Archer payout account
- **ruleId** — the identifier of the rule that authorized the transaction
- **sequence** — the sequential transaction number for ordering
- **recipient** — the destination for the transfer
- **amount** — the transferred amount
- **feeAmount** — the amount of the Archer service fee charged

## Bank transactions

ACH and wire transactions also appear in your transaction history. Each record shows the payment type (ACH or wire), recipient name, amount, and current status. Status values are:

- **Pending** — the payment order has been submitted and is awaiting settlement
- **Settled** — funds have been delivered to the recipient
- **Failed** — the payment could not be completed; check the error details or contact support

<Tip>
  Transaction history is suitable for accounting, tax reporting, and compliance audits. Use the date range filter and CSV export to generate records for any reporting period.
</Tip>
